Why the scoop counter falls back

Two separate mechanisms, both now identified from the rollout records. One was a rule bug and is fixed. The other is a label-design ambiguity at the tray, and it is the same kind of problem the light-state sentences solved for the blinks.

1 · A borderline write was lost foreverThe change detector compared against the last produced sentence. A first "scoop 2" at 0.90 confidence was rejected once and never retried. Fixed: retry until committed.
2 · Two moments, one input"Previous sentence = scoop 2, arm over the tray" happens while dumping scoop 1 and when arriving with scoop 2. Only the fill of the scoop differs.
Fix for 2 · sub-phase sentences"scoop k: to the tray" from the tray arrival, "scoop k: to the bowl" from leaving it. Every boundary sits on a persistent state and the previous sentence names the half-cycle.

1. Anatomy of a scoop cycle

The base joint of the right arm (j0) tells where the scoop is: below 0.75 it is over the white bowl (the bean source), above 0.8 it is over the glass tray. One cycle is dig → carry → dump → return. 6. What follows

Done

Retry-until-committed write rule (on for every new stage B and for serving). Tray-cut labels, which made the increment visible at all. Visible-LED labels, which removed the label-versus-camera lag. A5/B5 without the write delay, to close the single-step-blink count issue, running now.

Next for the scoops

Sub-phase sentences built from the same joint events (tray arrival, delivery end): 16 sentences instead of 14, one relabel script, one A/B round of about 5 hours on the 4×H100. The count-flip battery and these same four episodes are the acceptance test: every increment on time, no wobble, and demo21 held.
